EXPERIENCE

Crafting My First Portfolio Website

I would like to share a challenging yet rewarding experience of building my first personal portfolio website. I started this project on April 13, 2026, as a platform to showcase my school assignments at vocational high school (SMK). I call it challenging because building a website from scratch required much more time and patience than I initially expected.

The most memorable part of this journey was the design phase. I spent hours and even days working in Figma. I created the design while exploring various references from platforms such as TikTok, Google, Facebook, and portfolio websites made by my seniors. I kept adjusting the layout and color palette repeatedly. There were moments when I felt confused, as I wanted the website to look visually appealing while still being functional. Although it was sometimes frustrating, this design process became the most interesting part for me because I learned how to balance aesthetics with user needs.

After finalizing the design, the real challenge began during the coding phase using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The most difficult part for me was ensuring the website remained responsive on both laptop and mobile devices. I still remember spending hours focusing on fixing broken layouts and links. However, every time I successfully solved a problem, I felt an incredible sense of accomplishment that motivated me to keep going.

Finally, on May 14, 2026, I successfully uploaded the website online through GitHub. Even though it took weeks to complete, I felt very satisfied because I was able to finish it independently. Now, I feel more confident in seeking an internship opportunity (PKL). This experience is not just a school assignment, but a personal achievement that motivates me to continue growing in the field of web development.
